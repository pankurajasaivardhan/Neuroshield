AI models. Not a medical device. Not for medical diagnosis. Never upload real patient data - de-identified research scans only. Uploads are deleted within about an hour.

Model limits

Alzheimer's model

Correct on about 71% of 36 held-out test scans (3 training runs). It can be overconfident when wrong - the project audit found such cases. Trained only on OASIS data; other scanners or populations may behave differently.

Tumor model

Outlines the whole tumor with Dice 0.90 on 56 held-out cases, but depends heavily on the FLAIR sequence: remove it and most cases degrade sharply. Two model families disagreed with each other on every test case.
